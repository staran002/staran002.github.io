Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Sand Jar plays fully offline.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. There is no account or cloud save. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may restore your progress when you set up a new phone with the same account.

I reinstalled the game and my progress is gone.

Uninstalling deletes the game's data from the phone. If Android backup was on, reinstalling from Google Play on a phone signed in to the same Google account may restore it. We cannot restore progress for you, because we never receive it.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress. This clears levels, stars, coins, chapters and your shelf. It cannot be undone.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen or the pause menu.

Are there ads or purchases?

Not in the current test version. Ads and optional purchases may be added in later versions; the privacy policy will be updated first.

I am a closed tester. How do I report a bug?

Use the feedback option on the Sand Jar page in the Google Play app, or the feedback form from your invitation. Say what you did, what happened, and add the app version and player code from Settings → About. A screenshot helps a lot.

Is the game for children?

Sand Jar is made for teens and adults (13+). It is not directed at children.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your data.
