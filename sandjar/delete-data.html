Delete your Sand Jar data

Sand Jar by S&T Mobile Apps · Updated 16 November 2026

Sand Jar has no accounts, and we keep no game data on any server. All game data is on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time. You do not need to ask us.

Option 1: Reset progress (keeps the game installed)

  1. Open Sand Jar and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Reset progress at the bottom and confirm.

This deletes your levels, stars, coins, chapters, ornaments and shelf jars. Your settings (sound, music and so on) and your player code are kept. Use Option 2 or 3 to remove everything.

Option 2: Clear the app's storage

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Sand Jar.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (on some phones: Clear data).

This deletes all Sand Jar data on the phone: progress, shelf, settings, player code and the game log. The game starts fresh next time.

Option 3: Uninstall

Uninstalling Sand Jar deletes all of its data from the phone.

Backups in your Google account

If Android backup is on, a copy of your progress and settings may be kept in your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Google manages this copy, not us. To stop it, turn off backup for the app or your phone in your phone's backup settings (Settings → Google → Backup, names vary by phone), or manage your backups in Google Drive / Google One.

E-mails you sent us

If you contacted support, we have your e-mail. To have it deleted, write to us from the same address and say "please delete my e-mails". We will do it within 30 days.
