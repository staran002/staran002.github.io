Sand Jar privacy policy

Version 1 (closed test) · Effective 16 November 2026

In short: Sand Jar has no account and no sign-in. This version of the game does not collect personal data and does not send any data to us or to anyone else. Your progress is stored only on your phone. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking in this version.

Who we are

Sand Jar is made by S&T Mobile Apps, an independent developer based in Cyprus (European Union). We are the controller for any personal data described in this policy. Contact: see Contact below.

What the game stores on your phone

To work, the game saves some data in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and the game does not send it anywhere.

  • Game progress: levels finished, stars, coins earned in the game, unlocked chapters, ornaments, and the jars on your shelf (including small pictures of them).
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, motion and text size choices.
  • Player code: a random 6-character code made on your phone (shown in Settings → About). It is not linked to your name or device. It only leaves your phone if you choose to give it to us when asking for help.
  • Game log: a technical log of game events (for example "level 3 started", times, the phone's time zone offset, phone model, Android version and app version). It helps the game recover from problems. It stays in the app's storage, is never sent, cannot be exported from the store version of the app, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

What we collect

Nothing. This version of Sand Jar makes no network requests: it loads no ads, has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools (SDKs), and does not use any advertising ID. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The game plays fully offline.

The app's Android manifest includes the standard "internet" permission because the app framework we use (Capacitor) includes it by default. This version does not use it to send or receive any data.

Things you choose to do

Sharing a picture of your jar

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your jar and opens your phone's normal share menu. You choose where it goes (for example a messaging app). We do not receive the picture, and nothing is shared unless you tap Share and pick an app.

Links

Links in the game (such as this policy) open in your browser or the Google Play app. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Contacting us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write. If you use Contact support in the game, your e-mail app opens with a draft that already contains your phone model, Android version, app version and player code, so we can help faster. You can edit or delete that text before you send it. We use support e-mails only to answer you, keep them no longer than needed (at most 12 months after the conversation ends), and delete them sooner if you ask. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Google Play and Android

  • Google Play handles downloads and updates and may give us statistics such as install counts and anonymous crash and freeze reports ("Android vitals"), and, during the closed test, the feedback you choose to send through Google Play. Google processes this under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the game's progress and settings (not the game log) to your account so you can restore them on a new phone. Google handles this backup; we cannot see it. You control it in your phone's backup settings.

Children

Sand Jar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you think a child has sent us personal data (for example in an e-mail), contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold game data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete except support e-mails you sent us. You can delete all game data yourself at any time: see Delete your data. If you live in the EU/EEA or the UK you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. In Cyprus this is the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Planned changes

Sand Jar is in testing. Later test builds are planned to add:

  • Ads through Google AdMob, with a consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it, and
  • Optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, possibly managed with RevenueCat.

These will involve some data (for example an advertising ID and purchase records). We will update this policy before any build with these features is released, and the date at the top will change. This version 1 applies only to builds without ads or purchases.

Changes to this policy

We will post any new version on this page with a new effective date. For important changes we will also tell you in the game or in the store listing.
