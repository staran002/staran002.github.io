Sand Jar

A calm puzzle game for Android: pour coloured sand into a glass jar to recreate a tiny landscape, with only so much of each colour. Keep the jars you make on your own shelf. Short levels, one-thumb play, works offline.

Sand Jar is in a closed test on Google Play right now.
